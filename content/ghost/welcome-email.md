Thanks for subscribing.

I do not plan to crowd your inbox. One field note a week is enough.

The subjects will move around because men’s lives do. Strength and sex one week. Grief, work, friendship, ambition, fatherhood, shame, anger, or repair the next. Whatever adulthood has exposed and most of us were taught to keep moving past.

Some essays will be personal. Some will be practical. Some will begin with a question and end with a more expensive one.

The premise is simple: men are not broken, and we are not finished.

This work takes men seriously. That includes what hurts, what we want, what we fear, and what we are responsible for.

I am not a therapist, and this is not clinical advice. I am writing from the middle of the work, where understanding something and living it remain annoyingly different accomplishments.

Start with the first field note:

**Strength Has to Grow Up**

If you feel like it, reply and tell me what brought you here. A question is enough. You do not need to send the polished version.

I read every response.
