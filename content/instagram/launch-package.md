## 4. Static Text Post

### Graphic copy

**Some of what we called strength  
was fear with good posture.**

### Caption

The performance may have started for a good reason.

Staying composed kept the peace. Self-reliance prevented disappointment. Anger felt safer than fear. Being useful made us harder to abandon.

A strategy can save a boy and still limit the man he becomes.

Growing up means noticing when the old protection has become a prison.

#MensGrowth #SelfAwareness #GrownMenGrow

## 5. Direct-to-Camera Reel

### Script

I used to think composure was one of my best qualities.

Sometimes it was.

Other times I had just left the building emotionally while keeping my face normal.

That is the strange thing about masculine performance. It often uses real strengths. The discipline may be real. The calm may be real. The competence is usually very real.

None of that tells you what is happening underneath.

A man can look strong and still be hiding.

The people closest to him usually know the difference.

That is what I wrote about in the first Grown Men Grow field note.

It is called **Strength Has to Grow Up**.

## 7. Approved launch sequence

1. **Launch day:** Publish the pinned introduction carousel, pin it to the profile, and publish Story frames 1–3 with the tracked Start Here link.
2. **Day 1:** Publish the foundational carousel and pin it beside the introduction.
3. **Day 3:** Publish the recognition carousel and pin it as the third profile anchor.
4. **Day 5:** Publish the static text post.
5. **Day 7:** Publish the direct-to-camera Reel after its spoken audio, captions, cover crop, and profile-grid crop pass review.

The sequence may compress if launch timing requires it, but do not publish three feed carousels at once. Give each post enough time to collect meaningful saves, shares, comments, and profile visits.

## 10. Story and Reel accessibility

- Keep all Story text inside Instagram's safe area and verify it at phone size before posting.
- Add the approved link sticker label to Story frame 3 rather than leaving the placeholder visible.
- Use Instagram's poll and question stickers so their controls remain native and accessible.
- Add accurate automatic captions to the Reel, then manually correct every word before posting.
- Keep the spoken script available in the Reel caption or transcript surface where Instagram supports it.
- Do not rely on music to carry meaning. The direct voice and captions must work with sound off.
