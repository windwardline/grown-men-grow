# LinkedIn Page

Men are told, correctly, to open up. What nobody mentions is that disclosure carries a built-in reward — the exhale — and it arrives whether or not anything else happens.

That is the trap. A confession without follow-through is a withdrawal from someone else's account.

The new field note is about the difference between opening up and owning up: repair answers on the other person's timeline, names the behavior, volunteers the cost, and repeats. [canonical link]
