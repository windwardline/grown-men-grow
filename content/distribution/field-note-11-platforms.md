# LinkedIn Page

There is a switch in most buildings that have to keep running. It sits between the utility and the emergency panel, and when the power drops it throws, and the load lands somewhere else. Inside, one fluorescent tube stutters. The people at the desks keep typing. Nothing in the room announces that something outside is now burning fuel so the room can stay ordinary.

The same thing happens to men at work, and they generally do not hear it happen. A mistake gets absorbed. A line item moves. A deposit goes in late and the next job goes to somebody whose deposit was on time. Nobody says why, and the man supplies his own reason, and the one he supplies is usually worse than the true one and lasts a great deal longer.

The new field note is about the eleven days after an expensive mistake, and about finding out years later, sideways, at a retirement lunch. It does not argue that the switch always throws. Most panels have nothing behind them. [canonical link]
