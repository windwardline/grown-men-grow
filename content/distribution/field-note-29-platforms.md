# Bluesky

## Post 1

There's a contractor who only works nights. Around ten to four he sits on the end of the bed and prices the house: the roof, the retirement account, the two-line email from the boss. He's never once come in under. He's never been inside any of the buildings.

## Post 2

Anybody who bids work for a living learns early not to price a job over the phone. What he can't see goes in as contingency. A bid made from bed, in the dark, is mostly contingency with a few real numbers on top for decoration.

## Post 3 — canonical link

What the walk buys isn't a better number. It's a job instead of a dread. A job has a start date, an order of operations, and people whose trade it is. [canonical link]
