# LinkedIn Page

There is a rest model most driven men run without ever having chosen it: rest is what you get after. After the deadline, after the season, after the list — the list that has never once been finished in the recorded history of lists.

It sounds disciplined. It is actually just debt, deferred at interest. And the crash gets named something more respectable: a bad back, a short fuse, a weekend on the couch that fixed nothing.

The new field note is about the other model — maintenance: scheduled, not earned, by interval, not by feeling. The pit crew doesn't wait until the engine seizes. [canonical link]
