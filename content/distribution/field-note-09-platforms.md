# LinkedIn Page

Comparison has one honest use and one expensive misuse, and most careers run on the misuse.

Another man's title, trajectory, or shoulders at forty-six is real, measurable information. The error is treating it as directions. Passing one man, you ease off — ahead is ahead — though your own destination may still be far. Trailing another, you redline through territory that deserved slow attention.

The only comparison with identical conditions and complete information: you, against you, over time — in the ways you decided matter, not the ways that photograph well. The new field note is about demoting other men from map to survey data. [canonical link]
