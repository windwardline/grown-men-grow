# LinkedIn Page

Effort is the familiar part for a certain kind of man. He knows how to grind; grinding is home. Which is exactly why it gets used as a hiding place when the real question — toward what? — requires standing still to ask.

The compass questions fit in a normal working life: If this works completely, what do I have? Who set this course? What's the cost-per-mile lately? Some directions turn out to be inherited — a decision made by a 22-year-old who no longer exists but still holds the map.

The new field note is about the demotion of effort from answer to engine, and why turning is not quitting. [canonical link]
