# Editorial collage concept v1

Status: review concept only. Not authorized for publication, Ghost upload, or Instagram posting.

This set tests an original cut-paper editorial system across five launch surfaces:

- Ghost feature hero
- Instagram cover
- Carousel interior spread
- Story cover
- Social card

The system uses bright photographic composition studies as raw material, then adds visible paper, irregular cuts, tape, halftone, drawn lines, offset blocks, and changing type scale. Energy comes from composition and material contrast rather than darkness, distress, or grievance-coded imagery.

The layouts use only founder-approved Essay 1 copy. Instagram surfaces do not identify the founder. The Ghost feature retains the approved secondary byline hierarchy.

Source-image prompts and dispositions are recorded in `assets/source/editorial/README.md`.
