# Editorial direction v1

Concept-only assets for founder review. Nothing in this directory is approved for publication or posting.

## Direction

The system keeps the Grown Men Grow oxblood and warm paper palette, then adds original editorial photography, stronger display typography, asymmetric composition, image crops, issue notation, rules, grain, and materially different layouts by surface. Approved launch copy remains unchanged.

## Generated photograph

`essay-01-hero-source.png` was generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool as an original project-bound editorial source.

Prompt:

> Create a visually arresting, restrained editorial portrait about the tension between outward composure and unfinished inner work. In a spare, elegant but ordinary plaster-walled room, show an adult man in his early forties with medium-brown skin and a solid build, wearing a crisp white dress shirt with casually rolled sleeves. He sits upright on one hard wooden chair, composed, face turned partly away, hands together with a trace of tension rather than melodrama. Use hard late-afternoon side light, deep sculptural shadows, cinematic 35mm grain, tactile natural skin and fabric, near-monochrome charcoal and warm ivory, with one subtle muted oxblood detail. Frame vertically at 4:5 with generous negative space above and left. Make it original high-end magazine editorial photography without copying any publication’s trade dress. No text, logos, borders, mastheads, watermarks, gym imagery, boxing, weapons, cigars, whiskey, luxury watches, sports cars, shirtless posing, alpha clichés, aggression, crying, therapy staging, or corporate-stock polish.
