# Bluesky

## Post 1

Getting dropped on a group ride happens politely. Nobody attacks. The group just climbs at the speed it rode the flat, and the wheel in front of him drifts off into another county.

## Post 2

A sweep hears "go on, I'm fine" about once a ride. Going on ahead isn't something the job contains. A sweep who goes on ahead is just a man who left somebody on a hill and went for coffee.

# LinkedIn Page

Every no-drop ride has a sweep, or says it does. The ride leader sits at the front and knows the route. The sweep sits at the back and knows one more thing: where the last man is at all times, because the last man is the job.

The new field note follows a group ride from the back. It's straight about the regroup that hands the slowest rider forty seconds of rest, and about the rides that turn out to be no-drop for the first ten miles. [canonical link]
