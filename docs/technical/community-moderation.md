# Community Moderation Playbook

Status: launch operating standard. Applies to Instagram and every approved public distribution surface.

## Purpose

Protect a serious adult conversation without sanitizing disagreement or using conflict as growth bait. Grown Men Grow is pro-male and responsibility-centered. It is not a grievance account, a gender-war venue, or a substitute for private clinical support.

## Decision table

| Comment or message | Action |
|---|---|
| Good-faith disagreement, criticism, or correction | Leave it. Reply only when there is something useful to add. Correct factual errors plainly. |
| Personal story offered without demanding advice | Acknowledge it without diagnosing, extracting details, or turning it into content. |
| Misogyny, anti-male dehumanization, racial or sexual slurs, threats, harassment, or celebration of abuse | Remove. Use the platform's restrict, block, or report control as proportionate. Do not debate it for reach. |
| Manosphere, red-pill, incel, or gender-war recruitment | Remove when it recruits, dehumanizes, or tries to turn the account into an ideological venue. A good-faith question may remain. |
| Repetitive bait, spam, impersonation, scams, or unsolicited promotion | Remove and restrict or block repeated behavior. |
| Credible self-harm statement | Do not attempt counseling in public. Report the content through the platform's suicide or self-injury route. If the founder personally knows who and where the person is and believes the danger is immediate, contact local emergency services. Do not store private details in the repository. |
| Credible threat toward another person | Do not debate, investigate, or amplify it. Preserve only what the platform or law enforcement requires, use the platform's threat-reporting route, and contact local emergency services when a specific immediate danger is personally identifiable. Do not store private details in the repository. |
| Private request for therapy, legal advice, medical advice, or crisis support | State the boundary and direct the person to qualified local help. Do not continue as a private adviser. |

## Reply standard

- Write in the same restrained, adult voice as the publication.
- Answer the actual point. Do not perform warmth or authority.
- Apply `docs/editorial-underpinning.md` in replies, where it bites hardest: take the person's pain seriously rather than smoothing it over, and do not tell a stranger what to do with his life. Reader replies are also evidence about how the writing lands — carry recurring objections and questions back to the founder rather than answering them one at a time forever.
- Do not thank someone for abuse, manufacture consensus, or turn every criticism into a lesson.
- Do not diagnose a commenter, a former partner, a public figure, or a demographic group.
- Do not expose private messages or use a reader's disclosure as content without explicit informed permission.
- Admit a clear mistake and correct it. Do not bury a correction in argument.
- End an exchange when it stops producing understanding. The account does not owe every stranger a debate.

## Boundary replies

Use these as starting points, not mandatory canned language.

**Private-advice boundary**

> I read this. I cannot offer private clinical, legal, or crisis advice. Please take it to someone qualified to help where you are.

**Conversation boundary**

> I understand your point. We are repeating ourselves, so I am going to leave it there.

**Correction**

> You are right about that detail. I corrected it.

## Operating cadence

- Check launch comments and message requests more frequently during the first 24 hours, then at a sustainable daily cadence.
- Use native filters for obvious slurs and spam, but review context before removing ordinary disagreement.
- Record aggregate moderation patterns in the handoff log without usernames, message text, or personal details.
- Escalate a policy change to the founder. Do not quietly widen what the account removes.

Instagram's reporting flow includes dedicated categories for suicide or self-injury, violence, hate, exploitation, bullying, scams, and spam. Use the native category that fits; this account is not a crisis-assessment service.
