# Ghost + Instagram Launch Implementation Plan

Status: Approved on 2026-08-07

## Objective

Launch the smallest reliable publication system that preserves the established editorial, reputation, and platform controls.

## Required launch system

1. A local Git repository under `/Users/peacock/Projects/`.
2. A managed Ghost publication with a custom domain.
3. An official Ghost theme with minimal configuration.
4. Public Start Here and About pages.
5. Free membership and one newsletter.
6. One automated free-member welcome email.
7. One public launch essay.
8. Native Ghost analytics and signup attribution.
9. One public Instagram Creator account with native Insights.
10. Manual backup/export and release procedures.

## Excluded from launch

- Additional social or publishing platforms.
- Paid memberships or Stripe.
- Third-party analytics, pixels, tag managers, or link shorteners.
- External email platforms.
- Automation services.
- Databases, custom member tools, or custom applications.
- Custom Ghost themes unless the official theme cannot satisfy a launch requirement.

## Delivery stages

### 1. Local foundation

- Initialize the repository on a non-default working branch.
- Exclude imported confidential material, credentials, and data exports from Git.
- Create the decision log, setup runbook, analytics plan, and release checklist.

### 2. Founder decisions

- Finalize the publication name, domain, Instagram username, launch date, theme, and visual basics.
- Approve public account identity, support and reply-to addresses, metadata, and the Instagram bio.
- Approve the Ghost subscription level after current feature and price confirmation.

### 3. Private Ghost staging

- Create the managed Ghost publication.
- Keep the publication private during setup and review.
- Configure general settings, theme, free membership, Portal, one newsletter, and the welcome email.
- Transfer approved launch copy without editorial rewriting.
- Preview web, mobile, email, and social presentation.

### 4. Release preparation

- Configure and verify the custom domain only after approval.
- Configure Instagram as a Creator account before launch posts are published.
- Complete editorial, reputation, responsive, email, link, metadata, accessibility, and analytics checks.
- Obtain explicit production authorization.

### 5. Launch and verification

- Publish Ghost pages and Essay 1.
- Send Essay 1 only if separately authorized.
- Publish the approved Instagram launch sequence manually.
- Verify live signup, welcome email, attribution, social previews, and analytics.
- Create a dated export set and capture day-0, day-7, and day-30 metrics.

## Change control

Any platform, paid service, automation, custom application, database, public-copy change, or disclosure change requires a separate written proposal and founder approval.
